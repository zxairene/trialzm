    01/ the poem i wrote : )

    The first time I met you, I won’t lie,
    I fell for you because you were beautiful.

    Your eyes caught my attention first,
    those eyes I could probably stare at for hours.
    Then your smile, your laugh,
    your height, your face,
    even your stupid jokes that weren’t even that funny,
    but somehow made me smile anyway.

    At first, I thought that was all it was.
    I thought I just liked the way you looked,
    the way you smiled,
    the way you made me feel whenever you were around.

    But then I got to know you.

    And the more I did,
    the more I realized there was so much more to you
    than what everyone else gets to see.

    You have so much light in you.
    So much love.
    So much to give.

    And I noticed how you could carry yourself
    even on the days when you were struggling.
    You could be hurting,
    you could be tired,
    you could be falling apart inside,
    and somehow, you still wouldn't let anyone see it.

    I don't know if you realize how strong that makes you.

    And that's when I knew
    I didn't just love your face anymore.

    I loved you.

    Not the version of you that everyone sees,
    not just the tall, handsome guy with the pretty eyes and charming smile.

    I loved the person behind all of that.

    The person who laughs.
    The person who makes terrible jokes.
    The person who gets tired.
    The person who sometimes hurts
    but keeps going anyway.

    And God knows how much I love you.

    I wish I could put it into words,
    but even this isn't enough.

    Maybe you were never meant to be mine.
    Maybe I wasn't enough for you.
    Or maybe I was,
    but you just never saw me that way.

    Maybe when you looked at me,
    you never saw someone
    you could spend forever with.

    And honestly,
    I think I've learned to accept that.

    I'm not asking you to choose me.
    I'm not asking you to love me back.
    I'm not even asking you to stay.

    If anything,
    I just want you to be happy.

    I want you to be free.
    I want you to laugh without pretending.
    I want you to love without being afraid.
    I want you to find someone
    who sees you the way I do—
    someone who sees everything you try to hide
    and loves you anyway.

    And maybe that person won't be me.

    I know that hurts.

    But if loving you means
    wanting what's best for you
    even when what's best for you isn't me,
    then I guess I'll learn to love you that way.

    Because I loved you for your smile at first,
    but I fell in love with everything underneath it.

    And even if I never get to call you mine,
    I'll still be thankful
    that I got to know you,
    that I got to see the parts of you
    that you don't show everyone,

    and that, for a while,
    I got to love you.
